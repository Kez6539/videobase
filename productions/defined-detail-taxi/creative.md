## Why this direction
The owner came to collect his car, but chooses a taxi to preserve its freshly detailed finish. The final line reveals a concrete, absurd decision. The clean result causes the joke.

Three agents audited the creative, actual CTN sources and production pipeline. Creative review and reference review preferred the behavioural reversal to the white-suit/Me exchange. One critic noted the ordinary-clothes first frame is less immediately absurd than CTN's veiled binder or cake. The live performance must earn it. The white suit was removed because it explained the ending too early and duplicated the joke.

The own-car purchase idea was discarded because it repeated Kitchen's Next. The facial-reflection joke was discarded for difficult image physics and an insulting service interaction. Blue. Allegedly remains an earlier approved, unproduced script, not the selected new scene.
